How to use it

Warm, not hot. Low and steady.

  1. 1

    Put it on early

    Reach for it at the first cramp, not when the ache is at its worst. Place it on your lower belly or lower back, wherever you feel it.

  2. 2

    Begin on a low level

    Keep a layer of clothing between the belt and your skin. If it stings or your skin goes red, turn it down.

  3. 3

    Keep it gentle

    A comfortable, steady warmth is the goal. Add vibration massage if you like it, and take breaks whenever you want.

A few safety notes

  • Don't sleep while wearing it. Take it off before you nap or go to bed.
  • Don't use it on broken, irritated or numb skin.
  • If you're pregnant, ask your doctor before using it.
  • If your cramps are severe, new, or getting worse, please see a doctor.
